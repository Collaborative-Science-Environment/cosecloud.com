Who we work with

University and government research groups

COSE instruments are used by university and government research groups in the United States and India, with further international deliveries under way. Current applications include cell and tissue culture, plant gravitropism, small-organism models and ground controls for spaceflight experiments.

Collaborative Science Environment Inc. is a Wisconsin benefit corporation. Our control software is open source under the GPL, customers receive CAD files for sample fixtures, and every instrument ships with full technical documentation. About the company →
