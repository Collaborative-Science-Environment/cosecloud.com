Two-axis clinostat

Simulated microgravity, on the bench.

The SciSpinner Max rotates a sample about two independent axes so the time-averaged gravity vector tends toward zero. It fits inside a CO2 incubator, measures what the sample experienced, and runs for weeks.

Who uses it

Gravitational biology labs, on Earth

SciSpinner clinostats run in university and government laboratories across the United States, and are now delivered to research institutes in India and Brazil. They are used for cell and tissue culture, plant gravitropism, small-organism models and ground controls for spaceflight experiments.
