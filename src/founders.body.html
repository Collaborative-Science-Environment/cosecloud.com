About CoSE

A small company that builds research instruments everyone can use.

Collaborative Science Environment Inc. is a Wisconsin benefit corporation based in Madison. We design, print, assemble and test every SciSpinner ourselves, publish the control software under the GPL, and ship each unit with the same documentation our engineers work from.

Founders

How CoSE started

CoSE was founded by Jerry Miao, Richard Barker and Travis Tangen, who met at the Wisconsin Institutes for Discovery and took first place at a WARF Education Start-Up Weekend with a plan to put real research instruments in classrooms. The first product was FlashLapse, a time-lapse imaging system built for summer camps; the two-axis clinostat followed and became the SciSpinner Max.

Travis Tangen, a science educator who led the company’s classroom and outreach work, has since moved on. The benefit-corporation mission he helped write remains in the charter.
