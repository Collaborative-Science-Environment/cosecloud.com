date: 2019-06-12
author: Jerry Miao

summary: COSE's first sale was a custom two-axis clinostat for NASA Ames Research Center in June 2019.

![Three early SciSpinner clinostats side by side, each with a different frame and sample holder](/assets/news/early-clinostats.jpg)

In June 2019 COSE made its first sale: a custom two-axis clinostat for NASA Ames Research Center, with a chamber modified for the samples they wanted to run.

Two more followed that year, for the University of Wisconsin–Madison and North Carolina State University. Each of these early units was built to order, as the photo shows, and what we learned from them became the SciSpinner Max.
