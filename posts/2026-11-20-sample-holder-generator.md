title: New tool: sample holder generator
date: 2026-11-20
summary: A browser tool that generates a printable SciSpinner Max sample holder plate with a two-stage cut-out and optional magnet pockets, and downloads it as an STL.
tag: tools

The [Tools page](/tools/) has a new entry: the [sample holder generator](/tools/sample-holder).

It starts from the standard SciSpinner Max holder plate, with the same outline, sample recess and mounting holes, and adds a two-stage cut-out in the centre. Stage 1 is a pocket in the recess floor that leaves a ledge for the sample to rest on; stage 2 is an opening through the plate inside it. Each can be a circle or a rounded rectangle of any size that fits, and making them the same gives a single straight cut. If stage 1 is made smaller than stage 2, stage 2 shrinks to fit.

The plate can also include four sealed pockets for 8 × 3 mm disc magnets. Pause the print at Z 3.60 mm to drop them in.

The STL file name records the settings, for example `sample-holder_S1-circ60-d1.7_S2-circ50_mag.stl`. Print the plate flat with the recess facing up; it needs no supports.

Like the other tools, it runs entirely in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
