COSE will be exhibiting at the [42nd annual meeting of the American Society for Gravitational and Space Research](https://asgsr.org/) (ASGSR), 2–5 December 2026 at the Crystal Gateway Marriott in Crystal City, Arlington, Virginia.

Jerry and Richard will be at the booth with the current SciSpinner Max, running, so you can see the instrument, the touchscreen interface and the live gravity readout for yourself. There will be free goodies on the table while they last.

If you are running a SciSpinner, considering one, or want to talk through a simulated-microgravity experiment, this is the easiest place to meet us in person. Write to `info@cosecloud.com` before the meeting to set a time, or find us in the exhibit hall. The booth number will be added here when it is assigned.
