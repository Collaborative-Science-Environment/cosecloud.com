How this is calculated

The sample frame turns with the core, which turns inside the frame. With frame speed ωo and core speed ωi, a sensor at the centre reads the gravity direction g = (sin ωot · cos ωit, sin ωot · sin ωit, cos ωot), Clary et al. (2022) Eq. 1, the same kinematic model the g-level analysis tool compares runs against. The sensor is sampled at 20 Hz; ⟨g⟩ is the magnitude of the mean of one-second means over each trailing window, the instrument's own calculation. A real run adds the sensor's small zero-g offset, which the instrument and the analysis tool measure and remove. Coverage counts 288 equal-area cells of the unit sphere (12 bands in sin(latitude) × 24 in longitude) visited by the gravity direction.
