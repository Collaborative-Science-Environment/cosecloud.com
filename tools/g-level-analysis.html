Method

Each raw sample is ameas = atrue + b, where atrue is gravity in the rotating sensor frame (|atrue| = 1 g) and b is the accelerometer's fixed zero-g offset. During a run atrue sweeps the unit sphere, so the raw samples lie on a sphere of radius ≈ 1 g centred at b. The centre is found by linear least squares (|a|² = 2 b·a + (r² − |b|²)); an axis-aligned ellipsoid fit gives per-axis scale factors. b is subtracted from every sample before averaging. ⟨g⟩ is the magnitude of the vector mean over one-second block means (identical to the instrument's calculation). The kinematic model is Clary et al. (2022) Eq. 1 evaluated at the run's speeds with no sensor. Coverage counts 288 equal-area cells (12 bands in sin(lat) × 24 in longitude) visited by the corrected, normalised samples.
